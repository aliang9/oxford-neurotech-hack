How it works

Three steps, repeated every 30 seconds through the night.

1

Track

The headset scores each 30-second stretch of EEG as a probability for each of five stages: awake, N1, N2, deep N3, and REM. Uncertain readings stay uncertain; nothing is rounded off.

2

Forecast

A model of how sleep moves between stages, trained on 121 nights, combines those readings with how long you have been in your current stage and how far into the night you are. It predicts the next hour.

3

Decide

Inside your wake-up window it asks: is waking now better than waiting for a lighter moment? It weighs grogginess against lost sleep and checks again 30 seconds later. At your latest time it always rings.
