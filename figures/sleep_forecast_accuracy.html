Accuracy is the share of predictions where the model's most likely stage (wake, N1, N2, deep sleep N3, or REM) matched the stage scored by sleep experts. Tested on 153 nights from 78 people in the public Sleep-EDF dataset, each scored by a model that never saw that person (5-fold cross-validation). The model was given expert-scored stages up to the moment of prediction. With stages read live from a headset, accuracy will be lower.
